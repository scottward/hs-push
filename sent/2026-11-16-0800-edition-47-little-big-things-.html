The Coaching

Any serious methodology for executing big goals will require you to pay attention to a lot of little details. HabitStack certainly does.

Write clear, measurable goals.

Document the “why” behind them.

Track your progress and communicate your confidence levels to your team.

Create an action plan and keep it up to date.

Plan your week.

Plan your day.

Time-block your strategic work.

All those little things can feel annoying. They can feel tedious. Boring.

But you’ve got to do them.

The little things make the big things happen.

The Habits

☐ Pause to remember what your big goals are (don't skip this!).
☐ Review your actions from last week (done? not done?).
☐ Decide what actions you'll take this week (specific and measurable).
☐ Schedule the work in your calendar (time blocking).
