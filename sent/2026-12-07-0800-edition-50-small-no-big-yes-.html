The Quote

"If you focus on everything, you'll focus on nothing."
— Amy Hoy

The Coaching

Slow down for a second.

Read this carefully:

Focus. Is. Magic.

Pure leverage.

And, you’re wasting it.

How do I know?

Because 99.9% of people are trying to do too many things.

Focus requires courage, humility, and vigilance.

It is a habit that you build over time, not all at once.

What is one obligation or opportunity that you can let go of in order to focus more deeply on a goal that is more important?

Don’t say “everything is important” because that dooms your larger goals.

Let a small ‘no’ protect your bigger ‘yes’.

The Smile

The Habits

☐ Pause to remember what your big goals are (don't skip this!).
☐ Review your actions from last week (done? not done?).
☐ Decide what actions you'll take this week (specific and measurable).
☐ Schedule the work in your calendar (time blocking).

Now, you're set to win. 👍
